Step descriptions

Every recorded step gets a written instruction. Without an LLM, a simple template writes them. With an LLM, they read like a real tutorial.

Connection

When to write

Each step is described as it is captured. Steps it could not describe in time (for example because of the free-tier rate limit) are filled in when the recording ends.

Privacy

Anthropic and OpenAI-compatible providers: only scrubbed step text and interaction metadata are sent, never screenshots, raw OCR text, or typed values. A second privacy check runs before every request. Cloud text endpoints receive this scrubbed text; a local endpoint keeps it on this machine.

Gemini: scrubbed step text and redacted screenshots leave this device and go to Google. Raw screenshots are never sent. Redaction relies on OCR and can miss photos, unreadable text, or other information.

On Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, human reviewers may read it, and Google says not to submit sensitive or personal information. Review the provider's current terms before enabling this option.

Voice narration

Speak while you click. Your words are turned into text with Whisper (on Groq, using your Groq API key above), matched to the step you were on, and used to write that step. Tips you mention, like "only ZIP and PDF files are accepted", go into a Note on the step.

The transcript is scrubbed like the screen text. The audio recording itself is not scrubbed: it is kept with the guide and included in the ZIP export. Do not say passwords out loud.

Click "Test microphone" and say a few words. The bar should move while you talk.

Changing this re-matches a guide the next time you click Generate manual (no new transcription needed).
